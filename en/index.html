Egida – Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026. This is a translation of the Italian policy; if the two differ, the Italian version prevails.

Who we are

Egida is an Android app that helps you understand and keep track of your household paperwork (bills, fines, letters, pagoPA notices). The data controller is Giuseppe Torrisi. For any privacy request, write to domo.assistenza@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your documents stay on your phone. Egida does not ask you to create an account and has no online archive of your documents.

The following is saved on your phone:

  • your documents (title, sender, summary, amount, due date, pagoPA codes) in an app file, together with the information Egida derives from them: the rights found by the Shield, whether a contract renews automatically and the date to cancel it, and whether an expense can go in the 730 tax return (category, amount, date, presence of the tax code, payment method);
  • the photos and PDFs you scan or import, in an app folder;
  • your settings and an anonymous installation code, created at random on first use (see below);
  • what Egida learns from your corrections ("What I've learned"): for example that a supplier's bills belong to a certain person, how often they arrive or whether they are paid by direct debit. You can see it and erase it at any time from Settings;
  • the "Household numbers" that Egida finds in your documents (POD, PDR, customer code, contract or policy number, number plate, the supplier's customer service number or PEC address) and the savings you confirm in the savings counter, with the day you mark a document as paid;
  • the readings of your electricity, gas, water and LPG meters ("Your meters"): the number, the day and the name you give the meter. Usage, estimated cost, comparison with estimated bills and the water leak check are calculated on your phone.
  • the letters you mark as sent ("Sent letters"): the day, how you sent them, the text and the outcome. Reply deadlines, compensation and the next step are calculated on your phone.

Other apps cannot read this data. If you uninstall Egida, it is deleted, unless you have a backup.

For "Are you paying too much?", Egida reads the consumption and the energy price from your electricity or gas bill and compares them on your phone with the reference prices it downloads from the Egida server (the regulated "servizi di tutela" prices published by ARERA, the Italian energy regulator). No data from your bills is sent to download them.

If you turn on fingerprint lock, the check is done by your phone (fingerprint, face or screen lock): Egida does not receive or keep your fingerprints or your PIN.

If you use "Tell us what you think", Egida sends the form's answers to the Egida server (the stars, whether you live in Italy or another country, what you tried and the text you write), the app language and the Egida and Android versions. The feedback is anonymous: it doesn't contain your name, your documents or the installation code (the code is only used to count at most 5 pieces of feedback a day, in a counter that resets daily). The server keeps it until it is copied to the developers' private archive on GitHub, usually within two hours, then deletes it. We use it only to improve the app. Please don't write personal data in the text. If you want a reply, you can email us from the form: you send that yourself, with your email app.

Expected bills, the monthly summary, the cases you link (for example a fine, an appeal and a receipt), warranty reminders, duplicates and "When can I throw it away?" (how long to keep each document) are also calculated on your phone, using the documents you already have: nothing is sent.

With "Empty the drawer" you scan several documents in a row: each one is sent for reading just as when you photograph a single document (see below), one at a time. Documents identical to ones you already have are not saved unless you ask, and their photos are deleted.

The 730 kit collects deductible expenses on your phone. These may include medical expenses, which say something about your health: they stay on your phone like your other documents. When you tap "Send PDF" or "Send text", Egida prepares the summary on your phone and you hand it to the app you choose (email, WhatsApp…): Egida does not send it to anyone.

There are two kinds of backup, and Egida never receives either of them:

  • Your phone's Google backup. If you have turned it on in Android settings, the system saves Egida's documents and settings (not the photos) to your Google account, and restores them when you install Egida on a new phone. Android does this, not Egida: the data goes to your own Google storage, which handles it under Google's rules, and Egida has no copy of it. You can turn it off in Android settings, or exclude Egida from the backup.
  • Egida's backup copy. This is a zip file with your documents, photos and meter readings that you create from Settings and keep wherever you like: Egida does not send it to anyone.

What is sent to read your documents

To explain a document, Egida sends it to the Egida server, which passes it to Anthropic's Claude artificial intelligence and returns the answer to you. This is the only case in which Egida sends the content of your documents to anyone, and it happens only when you ask it to read a document or a meter, ask a question or ask it to write a letter. (The Google backup described above is done by the Android system to your account, not by Egida.)

What is sent:

  • the images of the document you want read;
  • the photo of a meter, when you ask Egida to read it: only the number stays on your phone, and the photo is deleted right afterwards;
  • your questions and the document details needed to answer them (titles, amounts, dates), when you use "Ask Egida";
  • the details of a document (title, sender, amounts, dates, codes and the notes you write, never the photos), when you use "Reply for me" to have a letter written; for a reminder letter, also the date and an extract of the first letter;
  • the language chosen for the answer and the language of the app;
  • the anonymous installation code and the app version.

A document may contain personal data: name, address, tax code, amounts, sometimes health data (for example a letter from INPS). If you don't want a document to be sent, don't ask Egida to read it: you can still file it and use it as a reminder, but you have to fill it in by hand.

The AI can make mistakes. Always check the amount and due date on the original document before paying. If you find a mistake, use "Report an AI error".

The Egida server

The Egida server runs on Cloudflare. It acts as a go-between: it receives the document or question, sends it to Claude and returns the answer to you. It does not keep photos, document texts or answers.

To offer a number of free readings each month and prevent abuse, the server keeps only:

  • the anonymous installation code, with the number of readings and questions in the current month. The code is created at random by the app, does not contain your name or any phone data, and the count resets every month;
  • an encrypted fingerprint of the IP address (not the address itself), with the number of requests that day, deleted the next day.

When you open Egida, the app asks the server whether Egida Plus is active for your installation (including when it is a gift, for example while the app is being tested). This request sends only the anonymous installation code and the app version: no documents and no personal data, and it does not count as a reading.

Cloudflare also logs technical request data for a few days (for example date, time, result and IP address), which is needed to run the service and find faults.

The legal basis is the performance of the service you ask for (reading the document or answering the question) and, for the anti-abuse limits, the legitimate interest in keeping the service available to everyone.

Who processes data on behalf of Egida

  • Anthropic (United States), which provides the Claude artificial intelligence. Anthropic processes data on behalf of Egida under its commercial terms and the related data processing agreement, and does not use data sent through its commercial APIs to train its models. Privacy policy: anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • Cloudflare (United States), which hosts the Egida server. Privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.
  • GitHub (United States), where the developers keep the anonymous feedback sent from "Tell us what you think" in a private archive. Privacy policy: GitHub Privacy Statement.

These providers also process data outside the European Union, with the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

Permissions requested

Egida only asks for the permissions needed for a specific feature.

  • Internet: to send documents to the Egida server when you ask for them to be read, and to open the pagoPA website when you choose "Pay with pagoPA".
  • Notifications: for reminders about due dates and about cancelling contracts that renew automatically, for the reminder one month before a warranty ends, for the notice when a sent letter gets no reply within the deadline, for the Sunday and monthly summaries (which you can turn off in Settings) and, if you turn it on, for the monthly reminder to read a meter. Reminders are prepared on your phone, without internet.
  • Calendar (read and write): only if you turn on syncing. Egida creates all-day events with the document's title and description. The events go into the calendar you choose: if that calendar is synced with an account (for example Google), the event follows that account's rules.

Egida does not ask for permission to use the camera or microphone. Document scanning is done by Google's scanner (ML Kit), which uses the camera on your behalf. Dictation in "Ask Egida" is handled by your phone's speech recognition, and "Read it to me" uses your phone's text-to-speech. Egida does not record or keep any audio.

Egida does not read other apps' notifications, your contacts, your location or other information on your phone.

Other services involved

  • Google's scanner and QR reader (ML Kit): document scanning and reading the pagoPA QR code happen on your phone, without internet. They are Google Play Services components, which may collect technical data under Google's own policy.
  • pagoPA: when you tap "Pay with pagoPA", Egida opens the checkout.pagopa.it website in your browser with the notice code and the organisation's tax code already filled in. From then on, the payment is between you and pagoPA. Egida does not see or keep your payment details: when you come back to the app it only asks whether you paid, to mark the document.
  • Google Play: you download and update the app through Google Play, which handles purchase data under its own rules.

Egida contains no advertising, uses no analytics services and does not sell or share your data.

How to delete your Egida data

Egida has no accounts: almost all data is on your phone and you delete it yourself.

  1. A single document: open the document and tap "Delete this document". Its photos are deleted too.
  2. All documents and settings: uninstall Egida, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Egida → Storage → Clear data. This deletes documents, photos, settings and the anonymous installation code.
  3. The Google backup: if Android backup is on, a copy of documents and settings (without photos) is in your Google account. Delete it from Google Drive → Backups, or from your phone's backup settings.
  4. Zip backup copies: these are files you saved yourself: delete them from wherever you put them.
  5. On the Egida server no documents, photos or texts remain. Only the monthly count linked to the anonymous code remains, and it is not linked to your name: after uninstalling, the code is no longer used. The IP address fingerprint is deleted the next day.

For any other deletion request, write to domo.assistenza@gmail.com with the subject "Egida data deletion": we reply within 30 days.

Your rights and data retention

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete a single document with "Delete this document" or delete everything by uninstalling Egida. The Egida server does not keep the documents it receives. The reading count resets every month and the IP address fingerprint every day.

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, take it with you (the zip backup also serves this purpose), restrict its processing and object to it. To exercise these rights, or for any clarification, write to domo.assistenza@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it).

Egida is not intended for minors. If we update this policy, the new version will be published at the same address, with the date of the last change.

AI errors and contact

Egida's explanations are generated by artificial intelligence and may contain errors in amounts, due dates or codes. They are not legal, tax or financial advice: always check the original document before paying.

If you find a mistake, tap "Report an AI error" in the document details or in "Ask Egida": your email app opens with a ready-made message, which you send only if you want to. For anything else, write to domo.assistenza@gmail.com.
